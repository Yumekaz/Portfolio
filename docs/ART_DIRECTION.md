# Sculptural artwork

Generated specifically for this portfolio using the built-in image generation
tool on October 18, 2026. These are expressive illustrations, not diagrams,
screenshots, or claims about physical hardware.

## Shared direction

Premium studio sculpture, tactile physically plausible materials, editorial
composition, no text, no logos, no UI, landscape 3:2. Restrained chrome,
vermilion, amber, warm stone, and charcoal connect the three pieces.

## Prompt briefs and assets

- **Coordination:** interlocking polished chrome ribbon structure surrounding
  three vermilion ceramic spheres; balanced tension, brushed-metal microtexture,
  soft warm-grey studio lighting. `public/art/coordination.webp` and
  `public/art/coordination-small.webp`.
- **Storage:** an exploded vertical stack of thick amber-glass and steel square
  slabs around a red-orange central axis; one offset layer, warm peach studio,
  precision material study. `public/art/storage.webp` and
  `public/art/storage-small.webp`.
- **Failure:** a fractured chrome torus suspended around a glowing vermilion
  orb; jagged broken metal faces, small fragments, high-contrast black studio,
  controlled explosion. `public/art/failforge.webp` and
  `public/art/failforge-small.webp`. Also used by the hero sculpture.

Each tool output was generated at 1536 x 1024. Sharp encoded full-size WebP
at quality 85 and 768-pixel variants at quality 82. No stock source, downloaded
third-party art, or fabricated attribution was used. CSS masks, clipped layers,
and transforms compose the hero; the source illustration itself is unchanged.
